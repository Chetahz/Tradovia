FAQ

Built for traders who want clarity.

Is Tradovia a signal service?

No. Tradovia is designed around journaling, analytics, risk and market context. TradingView technical summaries are shown as context, not Tradovia trade signals.

Can I still add trades manually?

Yes. Manual journaling remains a core workflow even if broker synchronization is added later.

Will Tradovia connect to MT5?

Read-only MT5 synchronization is on the product roadmap. The intended architecture uses backend-managed investor access rather than execution credentials.

Are the prices on the page final?

No. Plan structure is being designed first; Thai-market pricing will be locked before payment integration.

Does the interface support mobile?

Yes. The product direction includes responsive iOS and Android usage, with mobile-specific layouts for forms, navigation and trading tools.
